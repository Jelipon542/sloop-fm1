## 🎶 What Is sloop-fm1?

sloop-fm1 is software that runs on the M-VAVE FM-1. In simple terms, firmware is the software that tells a device how to behave. For the M-VAVE FM-1, sloop-fm1 turns the device into a live groovebox.

A groovebox is a music-making tool built around patterns. A pattern is a short repeated musical section, such as a drum beat or a bass line. Live groovebox use means you can play with those patterns while they are running. You can bring parts in, take parts out, change the feel, and shape the music as it happens.

sloop-fm1 is built around that idea. It is not a static editor where you stop the music every time you want to make a change. It is designed for movement. You can build a loop, let it run, and adjust the sound and structure as you go.

## 🎛️ Your First Live Groovebox Session

A good first session has three parts: a starting pattern, a small change, and a moment of listening.

Start by choosing a pattern. Keep it short. Four steps, eight steps, or whatever feels natural. The point is not to finish a song. The point is to have something running that you can react to.

Next, make one small change while the pattern is playing. This is the heart of sloop-fm1. Live groovebox work is about small adjustments in real time. You do not need to stop the music. You do not need to rebuild the pattern from scratch. You make a change, hear the result, and decide what to do next.

Finally, listen. Let the pattern run for a while without touching anything. This helps you hear what the groove actually sounds like. Many good ideas come from hearing a loop repeat and noticing what feels missing.

Repeat this process. Start, change, listen. Each time you do it, you will get faster. Each time, the FM-1 will feel more like an instrument and less like a piece of software.
